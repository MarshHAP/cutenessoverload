What we love

This is the one-piece we reach for on chilly mornings. The teddy fleece is thick and fluffy on the outside, soft cotton-blend jersey on the inside, and the hood ears are guaranteed to draw a smile at nursery drop-off. A full-length zip runs from the neck to the ankle, so there is no wrestling with poppers, and the enclosed feet mean no lost socks.

Materials, care & size

Outer: 100% recycled polyester teddy fleece.
Lining: 95% organic cotton, 5% elastane.

Machine wash at 30°C with similar colours. Do not tumble dry. Do not iron. Reshape while damp.
